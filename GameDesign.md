# Turtle Beach — Game Design Instructions

## Concept
A city-builder/roguelike game where you manage baby turtles that hatch from their nest.

## Core Gameplay
- Baby turtles hatch from a nest on an island.
- Hatched turtles collect resources.
- Resources are used to protect the unhatched siblings still in the nest.
- The nest is threatened by trash that washes in from the ocean.

## Setting
- The game takes place on an island.
